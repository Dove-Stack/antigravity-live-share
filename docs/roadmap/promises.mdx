## How voice & video work without new infrastructure

<Steps>
  1. **Signal over what exists**

      SDP offers, answers, and ICE candidates are just JSON frames — they ride
      the existing WebSocket relay exactly like `presence.cursor`.

  2. **Connect peer-to-peer**

      Each peer pair forms a WebRTC connection; audio and video never touch
      the server.

  3. **Degrade gracefully**

      No camera? Voice-only. No mic? Watch-only. The relay keeps working for
      text and presence regardless.
</Steps>

:::warning
Voice and video are **design-stage promises** — nothing in the repo yet. The
signaling schema below is the plan, not shipped code.
:::

## Access control, in three rules

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="1. Nobody reads before approval">
    Guests connect but receive no `doc.state` and no presence until the host
    approves — the gate sits in the client, before any content flows.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="2. Read-only is a server-side role">
    The relay drops `doc.update` frames from read-only members, so trust
    doesn't depend on client honesty.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="3. Room tokens, not obscurity">
    v0.2 sessions mint a share token alongside the ID; joining without a valid
    token requires host approval.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
